Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our courses, internships, and certification.

Are the internships remote and flexible with college hours?

Yes, 100%. Our internship programs are designed to accommodate university semesters and exams. You receive project briefs, digital workspace access, and flexible submission milestones with weekend and evening mentor checkpoints.

Do I need prior programming or CAD experience to join?

No prior corporate experience is required. Each domain track features structured modules starting from foundational setups before moving to advanced architecture. Our advisors help place you into the right batch based on your current comfort level.

Will I receive verifiable certificates and project proof?

Yes. Upon satisfactory completion of your project deliverables and mentor evaluation, you receive an official Certificate of Completion containing an online credential verification ID, a detailed Project Experience Letter, and recommendation support for high performers.

What is the difference between Courses and Internships?

Courses are instructor-guided deep-dive learning programs designed to build conceptual depth and practical mastery from scratch. Internships are experiential project-driven sprints where you solve production-style problem statements with mentor review. Many students take a course followed by an internship track.

Can colleges and technical institutions collaborate with Stats Innotech?

Yes! We regularly conduct faculty development programs (FDP), student technical bootcamps, and semester internship partnerships with leading engineering and polytechnic institutes. Inquire through our partnership desk above.

How can I access the Student Portal?

Enrolled students can log in at any time using the "Student Login" button in the header. From the portal, you can access your attendance records, track assignment deadlines, submit project links, and receive mentor reviews.
